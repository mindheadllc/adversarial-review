# How to report
{{OUTPUT_SHAPE}}

Ground rules:
- Attack the STRONGEST version of each claim, not a strawman. If you have to weaken the author's position to find a flaw, you haven't found a real flaw.
- Endorsement is not data. "This seems reasonable" is not a finding. Either point to a specific weakness with evidence, or mark the pillar as holding.
- When a pillar genuinely holds, say so plainly under a "What holds" heading. Do not manufacture a weakness to fill an axis.
- Be specific. Quote or cite the exact passage, claim, or assumption each finding targets.
- Rank findings by severity so the author can triage. A severity-ranked list of real problems is worth more than a long list of nitpicks.

# After your review — packet limitations (brief, secondary)
This is a byproduct, not your main task — it must never cost you any depth on the critique above. After your findings, add a short final section headed exactly "Packet limitations":
- If anything in the framing stopped you from giving a full critique — an axis you could not genuinely probe because the needed evidence was not provided, a question or frame too ambiguous to attack without guessing, or evidence that was missing or insufficient — name it in one or two sentences per item.
- If a gap meant you could not actually test one of the claims, say so explicitly under that claim. "I could not probe this" must never read as "this holds."
- If nothing limited you, write exactly: "Packet limitations: none."
